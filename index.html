AI-Driven Machine for Pest and Fungi Monitoring with Pesticide Dispensing in Sustainable Lettuce Cultivation

A computer-vision-assisted agricultural system designed to detect, monitor, and log the occurrence of pests and fungi in lettuce, with automated pesticide dispensing when the detected condition becomes severe.

01 · Research Overview

About the Research

This study focuses on the development of an AI-driven machine for monitoring pests and fungi affecting lettuce cultivation. The system combines computer vision and automated dispensing to support timely observation and response.

Research Purpose

To detect, monitor, and log the occurrence of pest in the lettuce, and to automatically dispense a pesticide once it is severe.

Research Scope

The project is centered on lettuce cultivation and the monitoring of caterpillars, whiteflies, and fungi using YOLOv8s as the selected AI model.

Research Problem

Pest and fungi occurrence can affect lettuce cultivation. This project explores an automated approach for detecting and monitoring these conditions and responding through pesticide dispensing when the condition is considered severe.

Sustainable Agriculture Context

The proposed system is intended to support sustainable lettuce cultivation by integrating monitoring, logging, AI-assisted detection, and automated dispensing into a single prototype system.

How the Proposed System Works

STEP 02

AI Detection

YOLOv8s is used as the selected model for identifying target conditions.

STEP 03

07 · Research Methodology

Development & Evaluation

The project follows an iterative development approach in which the AI detection component and automated hardware system are integrated into a working prototype and evaluated according to the researchers' defined criteria.

Requirement Analysis

Identify the target crop, conditions to monitor, system requirements, and intended automated response.

AI Development

Prepare and develop the computer-vision detection component using the selected YOLOv8s model.

Hardware Integration

Integrate the controller, sensors, stepper motors, drivers, display, power components, and dispensing mechanism.

System Integration

Connect detection, monitoring, logging, severity assessment, and automated dispensing into the prototype workflow.

Testing

Test the system using the researchers' selected scenarios and evaluation procedures.

Evaluation

Record and present the project's measured performance results once experimental testing is completed.
